A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Everything to know about the static demo, proposed free allowance and early access.

Is Community Circle available to use now?

Community Circle is currently in private beta. Use “Try the beta” to continue to the separate test application. Access and feature availability depend on testing. Sample dashboards on this website remain illustrative.

What does “up to 200 invited people” mean?

Up to 200 invited guest places per event, including plus-ones. One invitation for a household of four counts as four places. Exactly 200 is included; the allowance is based on invited places, not confirmed attendance.

What happens above 200 people?

Larger-event pricing will be announced later. No price, release date or automatic paid upgrade is promised.

Are suppliers free during beta?

Basic supplier listings are proposed to be free during beta, subject to onboarding and review. Optional premium plans may follow; future pricing has not been announced.

Does beta include bigger events?

Larger events may receive a free testing allowance by arrangement. No unlimited or permanent entitlement is promised.

Will you store my details?

This static prototype sends and stores no form entries. Use fictional details only. A real waitlist will need a connected service and a final privacy notice.

Can I collect payments or message guests?

No. These are proposed application workflows. This static site does not process payments, send emails or messages, or access your eventmanage data.
